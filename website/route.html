Route

The shortest route between any significant number of destinations is a surprisingly hard problem called the Traveling Salesman Problem or TSP. McTrot adds another bit of complexity in that not all McDonald’s are open 24 hours. The shortest route is no use if it leads to restaurants that aren’t open. This variation of the problem has been named TSP with Time Windows, or TSP+TW.

In practice, problems of this kind are often solved with a search technique, simulated annealing, that tries making random improvements to a random route. For McTrot, this is made possible by the restaurant locations and hours on the McDonald’s website and the NYC Office of Technology and Innovation’s sidewalk line dataset.

A McTrot route optimization tool using these techniques is available here.
