Privacy

In short: you can read McTrot without an account, and nothing tracks you. If you sign in to like or comment, we keep your email address, the name you choose, and what you post. Your email is never shown or sold. You can download everything or delete your account at any time.

Last updated November 19, 2026.

Who we are

McTrot (mctrot.nyc) is run by Wind Mountain LLC, New York, USA. We decide what happens to the information described here (in EU terms, we are the “controller”). Questions, requests and complaints: privacy@mctrot.nyc.

Reading McTrot

You don’t need an account to read the site, follow /live or listen to the radio. We use no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

Like any web server, ours record each request: your IP address, the time, the address requested and your browser’s name. We use these logs only to keep the service running and to deal with abuse, and delete them after 14 days. The website itself is served by Netlify, which keeps its own short-term logs.

Guest accounts

To like or comment on posts, you sign in with your email address: we email you a six-digit code, with no password. When you have an account, we keep:

  • Your email address, to send you sign-in codes. It is never shown to anyone.
  • The name you choose and an optional link, shown with your approved comments.
  • Your likes and comments, and when you made them.
  • That you said you’re 16 or older, and when.
  • Whether you asked for news emails, and when.
  • Your sign-ins: when each began and when it expires (90 days).
  • Reports you make about comments, until we’ve dealt with them.

We use these only to run your account, show your comments, show you your likes, and, if you opt in, email you news about McTrot, three times a year. We rely on running the service you signed up for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), on your consent for news emails (Art. 6(1)(a)), and on our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe for server logs and moderation (Art. 6(1)(f)).

What’s public

Every comment is read by us before it appears. Once approved, your comment, your name and your link are public on McTrot. Your likes are private: only you see them, and we don’t show how many likes a post has.

After the event, McTrot’s live page is kept as an archive on this site, and approved comments with their names and links may be part of it. If you delete a comment or close your account, we remove it from the archive too.

Cookies

Signing in sets one cookie on api.mctrot.nyc. It holds a random code that keeps you signed in, and nothing else. It is strictly necessary for your account, so it needs no consent. Signing out or closing your account deletes it.

Email

We send sign-in codes. We send news about McTrot, three times a year, only if you ticked the box for it; untick it on your account page, or email us, to stop. We never send marketing for anyone else.

Who else handles it

  • Mailgun (Sinch), from its EU region in Germany, sends our emails and so handles your email address and the messages.
  • DigitalOcean hosts our server and its database in New York, USA.
  • Netlify serves the website’s pages.

Each works under a data processing agreement with us. If you’re in the EU or UK, your information is stored in the USA. Those transfers are covered by the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses in those agreements.

We don’t sell or share your information with anyone else, or use it for advertising. We would hand it over only if the law required us to.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, comments and likes: until you delete them or close your account.
  • Accounts never finished (email confirmed but no name chosen): 24 hours.
  • Sign-in codes: 10 minutes. Sign-ins: 90 days, or until you sign out.
  • Server logs: 14 days.
  • Backups of the server’s disk are overwritten within 30 days, so deleted information is gone from them by then.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can:

  • Get a copy of everything about your account: “Download my data” on your account page gives you a JSON file.
  • Correct your name, link or email on your account page.
  • Delete a comment, or close your account, which deletes your account, comments and likes at once.
  • Withdraw consent for news emails at any time.
  • Ask us anything, object to how we use your information, or ask for something the pages don’t cover, at privacy@mctrot.nyc. We answer within 30 days.

If you’re in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority. We’d appreciate the chance to sort it out first.

Age

Accounts are for people 16 and older. If we learn that an account belongs to someone younger, we delete it. If you think that’s happened, tell us at privacy@mctrot.nyc.

Comments and reports

Be kind. We approve comments by hand and remove any that are abusive, hateful, illegal, spam, or share someone else’s private information, and we may close accounts that post them. Anyone can report a comment with its “Report” button, or by writing to privacy@mctrot.nyc, which is also our contact point for authorities and for the EU Digital Services Act. If we remove your comment, you can ask us why at the same address.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above, and email account holders about any change that matters to them before it takes effect.

Questions and new results: admin@mctrot.nyc.
