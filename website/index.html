Eat at every McDonald’s in Manhattan

McTrot is a continuously open, honor-system endurance challenge. A McTrotter visits every McDonald’s in the borough of Manhattan, eating one food item at each, on foot.

Self-supported with the obvious exception of eating at McDonald’s. No prize.

Plan a route. Read the rules. See the results.
