Eat at every McDonald’s in Manhattan, on foot, in one go

McTrot is a self-supported, honor-system endurance challenge. A McTrotter visits every McDonald’s in the borough of Manhattan, eating one food item at each, on foot.

Self-supported with the obvious exception of eating at McDonald's. No prize.

Plan a route. Read the rules. See the record.

Questions and challenges to the record: admin@mctrot.nyc.
