\textit{Recitals}

\medskip

Each year, McTrotter undertakes a personal challenge to eat at every McDonald's in Manhattan, on foot, in one go. In 2026, the challenge (``the Challenge'') will take place on November 20, 2026. McTrotter's goal is to complete the Challenge in under ten hours. In preparation for and in execution of the Challenge, McTrotter requires two pairs of athletic shoes---one for training and one for the event---each priced at approximately \$180. A total payment of \$500 covers the cost of both pairs of shoes, applicable sales tax, a tip to the shoe retailer employee, and the federal, state, and city income taxes McTrotter is required to pay on this income.

\medskip

\textit{Terms}

\medskip

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, label=\textbf{\arabic*.}]

\item \textbf{Benefactor's Obligation.} Upon signing this agreement, Benefactor will pay McTrotter \$500.

\item \textbf{McTrotter's Obligations.} McTrotter will use the payment to purchase two pairs of athletic shoes as described above, and will attempt the Challenge on November 20, 2026.

\item \textbf{Naming Rights.} McTrotter offers to include Benefactor's name and/or cause in publications related to the Challenge. Benefactor may specify preferences below or decline.

\medskip
Name/cause to include (optional): \underline{\hspace{6cm}}

\item \textbf{Exclusivity.} McTrotter will not seek or accept payment toward the purposes described above from any other party for this Challenge.

\item \textbf{Contingency.} In the event of injury or family emergency, McTrotter may postpone or cancel the Challenge. In either case, the payment is non-refundable and the shoes remain McTrotter's property.

\item \textbf{Copies.} This agreement has been executed in two signed copies, one retained by each party.

\item \textbf{Effectiveness.} This agreement becomes effective, and McTrotter's signed copy shall be delivered to Benefactor, only upon McTrotter's simultaneous receipt of the payment in full.

\end{enumerate}
